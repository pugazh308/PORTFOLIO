About

I'm a Data Engineer with hands-on experience across SQL, Snowflake, dbt, Python, Power BI and Microsoft Fabric. At Systech Solutions I've been building practical data engineering skills — working with source data, data modelling, ELT pipelines, data quality, and reporting.

What I enjoy most is understanding how data moves from the source to the final insight. I like working through each stage: understanding the data, designing the right structure, building transformations, and making sure the data is reliable and useful.

I recently earned the Microsoft Certified: Fabric Data Engineer Associate (DP-700) certification, which strengthened my grasp of modern data engineering and Microsoft Fabric. I'm always looking to learn, work on real-world data problems, and grow as a Data Engineer.
